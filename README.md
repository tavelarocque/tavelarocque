## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📊 [Implied Odds — Prediction Markets vs. Options](https://github.com/tavelarocque/implied-odds-research)
Live research system comparing Kalshi's S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and Bitcoin contracts with the probabilities implied by option prices (Breeden–Litzenberger on arbitrage-checked SVI smiles; Deribit for Bitcoin). A cloud pipeline on GitHub Actions collects Cboe, Kalshi, Polymarket and Deribit data around the clock, paper-trades the gap under fixed rules, and re-reads the order book after every trade to check it would really have filled. Every test is **pre-registered** before its data exists, judged on a locked holdout, and adjusted in a **multiple-testing ledger** (Holm / BHY). Results so far: Kalshi's S&P contracts are well calibrated, and a Kalshi + realized-volatility blend beat Kalshi's own price on the sealed holdout.
`Python` `NumPy` `pandas` `SciPy` `Options Pricing` `Bootstrap / Multiple Testing` `GitHub Actions`
[GitHub](https://github.com/tavelarocque/implied-odds-research)

### 🐫 [ocaml-contracts — A Contract Language for Prediction Markets](https://github.com/tavelarocque/ocaml-contracts)
A financial contract language in OCaml, after Peyton Jones, Eber and Seward's *Composing contracts*, that prices Kalshi's Bitcoin contracts from the probability distributions implied by Deribit options. Every contract is a tree of nine combinators, valued by **three independent pricers** that check each other (density integration, Carr–Madan static replication, a Rubinstein implied tree); they match Implied Odds' Python probabilities to **2.7 × 10⁻¹²** across 13,804 contracts. On top: bump-and-reprice Greeks, delta-hedging simulation, an **Avellaneda–Stoikov market maker** adapted to $0/$1 payouts, and a **markout system** for measuring how informed order flow is, all under 80 expect and property tests.
`OCaml` `Core` `dune` `Options Pricing` `Market Making` `Property Testing`
[GitHub](https://github.com/tavelarocque/ocaml-contracts)

### 💪 [MODUX — Training App for Hybrid Athletes](https://modux.ca)
A native iOS app for athletes who both lift and run, built solo end to end in 10 days and in App Store review. Its **Interference Score** models how strength and endurance training conflict: an **11-dimensional fatigue vector with 72-hour decay**, shown as a 0–100 readiness score (ACWR-based, with confidence scoring), with TRIMP-based training stress split between lifting and running load. It also has a per-muscle recovery map and syncs with Strava and Apple HealthKit, matching workouts across the two by fuzzy matching so none is counted twice, with a unique partial index enforcing it in the database.
`React Native` `Expo` `TypeScript` `Supabase Postgres` `Row Level Security` `Deno Edge Functions` `Strava API` `HealthKit`
[Website](https://modux.ca)
